GODFALL: THE FLESH WAR

Run: npm ci && npm run dev
Checks: npm run typecheck && npm test && npm run test:battles && npm run build
Browser checks (dev server on port 5173, or set GODFALL_URL): npm run test:browser
Browser tooling: npx playwright install chromium

MATCH RULES
Three playable factions fight a free-for-all against two independent commanders.
Destroy both rival factions' buildings to win; losing all your buildings is defeat.
Soldiers within 144px of the central remains advance their faction's ritual.
Two or more hosts in the circle pause all rituals. Progress is never lost.
After 180 uncontested seconds, the faction awakens permanently: double unit and
building health, 1.8x damage, and 20% faster movement. New recruits and buildings
inherit these bonuses. Ritual completion does NOT end the game. Every faction
can complete its own ritual to counter an awakened opponent.

FACTIONS
Humans: +20% unit health; E activates 8s of shield wall (half incoming damage,
half movement speed, 24s cooldown). Champions provide 15% damage and defense
bonuses within 100px. Towers have 30% more health.
Dark elves: +15% movement speed; E blinks soldiers up to 96px toward the cursor
through explored ground (18s cooldown). Forest edges conceal them unless an enemy
is within 80px or they attacked recently. Nightbows inflict 3 damage/s for 4s.
Corrupted: attacks have 25% shorter cooldowns. E activates 8s of frenzy (28s
cooldown): another 45% shorter attack cooldown, faster movement and uncontrolled
pursuit. New orders are ignored during frenzy. Nearby corpses restore 30 health;
three meals permanently increase maximum health and damage by 25%.

CONTROLS
Left click/drag: select. Right click: move, attack, gather, build, or rally.
Shift + command: queue up to 12 orders. Gathering runs until that node is depleted.
Q: attack-move; X: hold position; E: faction ability; I: idle worker; R: remains.
H: select hall; Space: center hall; WASD/arrows/edge hover/middle drag: pan.
Wheel: gentle cursor-centered zoom. Ctrl+1–9: assign groups; 1–9: recall.
Escape: cancel placement or attack-move, then pause. Backspace/Delete: stop.
A selection of only workers (one or many) offers Build; workers mixed with
soldiers get soldier commands. Halls and barracks cannot train until their
construction finishes. Surrender (pause menu) ends the match with the Defeat
screen and battle statistics.
The camera opens centred on your hall (as far as the map edge allows).
Touch: tap a unit, then Give order and tap a destination. Press-and-hold edge
arrows pan; unavailable directions disappear at the map boundary. Use the
command panel for attacks, abilities and building placement. Ritual status can
be expanded on narrow screens (collapsed by default); command buttons scroll
horizontally. Give order/Cancel sit in the playfield's bottom-right corner.

AI AND MAP
Three difficulty levels adjust reaction time, march timing, force size, retreat
threshold and ability use; there are no hidden resource multipliers.
Commanders contest the remains before invading, retreat wounded units, recover
near their hall, and expand toward fresh mines when local gold is exhausted.
Central approaches are cleared on every seed, with narrow forest flanks and
ruined structures around the objective. Construction leaves lanes between buildings.
Units use smaller collision radii and approach building facades at sub-tile positions.

SPRITES AND ANIMATION
All PNG artwork comes from /home/woez/Projects/shed-dev-sprites.
Regenerate with:
  /home/woez/Projects/shed-dev-sprites/.venv/bin/python scripts/generate_assets.py
Set SHED_SPRITES to override the repository location.
The generator's human animation engine supplies walk_pose and attack_pose for
four directions, eight frames per action, using frames=8 and align=False to
keep the rig anchored across frames. generator.sheet.make_sheet packs 32px
cells into 8-column transparent atlases. Rendering chooses rows from direction
and action, and columns from the animation clock. No generator changes or
runtime sprite service are required. Each of the 15 unit roles has 64 frames.
public/sprites/manifest.json records source, seeds, options and frame hashes;
atlas JSON sidecars record cell layouts. All 111 PNGs are pre-generated.
Canvas geometry handles trees, mine markers, selection UI and combat effects.

FONTS
The game makes no external requests. Cinzel and Cinzel Decorative (SIL OFL 1.1)
are self-hosted in public/fonts with credits and licence text; body text uses
the system sans-serif stack.

TESTS
npm test covers ritual contesting and counter-rituals, awakening inheritance,
shield/officer effects, shadow-step, concealment, poison, corpse mutation,
queued orders, hold position, central routes across seeds, economy round trips,
construction/training, building combat, multi-worker selection commands,
training blocked on unfinished buildings, and surrender.
npm run test:battles simulates three seeded battles with all factions and
all difficulty levels, requiring a result, ritual completion and viable economies.
Browser checks launch every faction, exercise pause/resume, verify phone HUD
fit, touch commands, and pan-arrow disappearance/reappearance at boundaries.
